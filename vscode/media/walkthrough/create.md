# Your own map

**Create Map** asks for a template and a title and saves a new map where you choose:

- **CANopen** — a bus and one device with its PDOs
- **SAE J1939** — an ECU with one PGN
- **CAN with explicit IDs** — a bus and one device
- **Empty** — just a bus

Then add devices, frames and signals with **+** in the Map view, or write the YAML: completion,
hovers and validation work as you type.
